Editions of this manuscript

A licenciatura thesis on the degeneracy of the symplectic form and on the intrinsic definition of second-order differential equations. The original is handwritten; what follows are four readings of it, from the most complete to the most faithful.

The manuscript translated and typeset, with an abstract, a plain-language account of the argument, an afterword on gauge theories and general relativity, a formal and an informal glossary, twenty-two diagrams redrawn after the author's own, references, and the seven manuscript pages in facsimile at the end.
  • Transcription — Portuguese The text as it was written, in the language it was written in, typeset and with the diagrams redrawn after the originals.
  • Transcription with the original diagrams The same Portuguese text, selectable, interleaved with the schemes and diagrams exactly as they appear in the manuscript.
  • Facsimile A self-contained HTML facsimile of the original, its pages embedded as images, preserving the handwriting, the symbols and the marginal notes as closely as possible.
